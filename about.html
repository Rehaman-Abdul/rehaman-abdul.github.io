About me

Pedagogy with a designer's eye.

I'm an instructional, multimedia and graphic designer who believes learning should be clear, human and beautiful.

My story

I'm Abdul Rehaman, a Senior Content Developer based in Bengaluru with 5+ years of experience creating user-centric learning and product content for SaaS and mobile platforms. I turn complex product functionality into how-to guides, eLearning, tutorial videos and knowledge libraries that people actually use.

At NoBroker.com I've produced 300+ feature tutorial videos that cut onboarding queries by 45%, run the company LMS (modules, onboarding, training needs analysis), and built an AI-powered WhatsApp support chatbot that reduced first response time by 80%. Before that I co-founded Suchandra Technologies, leading content strategy, video and SEO work for client apps.

I hold a B.E. in Telecommunication from RV College of Engineering and an Advanced Certificate in Digital Marketing and Communication from MICA. I work in English, Hindi and Kannada.

Design philosophy

  • Learner first. Every screen earns its place.
  • Clarity over decoration. Beauty that serves comprehension.
  • Measure it. If it can't be evaluated, it can't improve.
  • Accessible by default. Learning is for everyone.
