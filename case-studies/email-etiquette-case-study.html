Email Etiquette Challenge

A six-level, game-based training experience that helps corporate professionals write clearer, more professional emails.

The challenge

Unclear, curt or over-formal emails slow teams down and damage relationships, yet email etiquette is usually taught through a slide deck or a policy document that nobody remembers. The goal was to make practice active, quick and a little competitive.

The solution: six progressive levels

Level 1

Tone analysis

Classify snippets as Human & Professional, Robotic or Blunt.

Level 2

Intent classification

Sort emails into Request, Inform, Follow-up or Schedule.

Level 3

Email structure

Drag and reorder segments into a logical flow across three scenarios.

Level 4

Speed round

The same skills against a 15-second timer per question.

Level 5

Spot the mistake

Find the biggest etiquette violation in a realistic email.

Level 6

Subject line mastery

Choose clear, actionable subject lines.

Design decisions

  • Learn by doing. Learners classify, reorder and spot errors instead of reading rules.
  • Varied interactions. Multiple choice, drag-and-drop ordering, a timed round and error-spotting keep attention up.
  • Feedback on every answer. Each response explains why it is right or wrong, so mistakes become teaching moments.
  • Game mechanics. Points, a live score, a timer, confetti and a final rank (from Needs Improvement up to Communication Expert).
  • Realistic scenarios. Snippets mirror real workplace emails, including status updates, meeting requests and escalations.
  • Proof of completion. A results screen with level-by-level review and a printable report.
